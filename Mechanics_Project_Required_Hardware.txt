IMPORTANT NOTES:
- The 6WD chassis kit already includes the chassis, 6 BO motors and 6 wheels.
- Do not buy a caster wheel separately.
- The supplied BO motors are non-encoder motors, so encoder compatibility must be checked physically before purchasing a specific encoder.
- Do not buy TB6612FNG for this 6WD version; use 2 × BTS7960 as the planned motor-driver arrangement.
- Raspberry Pi 5 must receive a properly regulated 5 V supply. Do not connect the battery directly to the Pi.
- If the lab provides any component, do not purchase it separately.
